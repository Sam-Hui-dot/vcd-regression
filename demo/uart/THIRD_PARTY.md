# UART reference source

`uart_tx.v` is the unmodified MIT-licensed implementation by Alex Forencich.
Upstream revision is `1b867e53af738e4a8bc7c839ca2f1c07f40382dc`.
The license and copyright notice remain in the source.

Source https://github.com/alexforencich/verilog-uart/blob/1b867e53af738e4a8bc7c839ca2f1c07f40382dc/rtl/uart_tx.v

The local testbench transmits 0x55, 0x00, 0xff and 0xa6. The equivalent variant
replaces `DATA_WIDTH+1` with `1+DATA_WIDTH`. The fault variant drives the UART
stop bit low. Both variants are generated by the script.
